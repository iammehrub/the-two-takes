## Success measurement

Choose one initial measure and record its baseline before launch:
- Number of approved posts delivered on time
- Draft turnaround time
- Reach/engagement, if reliable analytics are available
- Qualified enquiries, if the client can measure them

Do not promise a specific increase. Report observed results with dates and source data.

## Suggested pilot scope

- Deliverable: seven caption drafts and a one-week content calendar.
- Exclusions: automatic publishing, paid ads, direct messages, fabricated testimonials, and unverified offers.
- Approval: every draft must be reviewed by the client before publication.
- Data handling: use only information needed for the agreed task; agree retention and deletion before collecting client data.
