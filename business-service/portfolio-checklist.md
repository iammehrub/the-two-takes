# Portfolio Checklist — Business Content Studio

## Before sharing a sample

- [ ] Label it **fictional sample** or **spec work** if no client commissioned it.
- [ ] Do not imply the sample business hired you.
- [ ] Use only confirmed business details or clearly fictional examples.
- [ ] Avoid invented prices, hours, offers, reviews, testimonials, awards, and results.
- [ ] Use original visuals or assets you have permission to use.
- [ ] Show the deliverable: 7 captions, calendar, visual direction, and review checklist.
- [ ] Include one short note explaining that every post remains a draft until client approval.
- [ ] Remove any private customer information.

## A simple portfolio structure

1. **Cover:** Business Content Studio — social content preparation.
2. **Brief:** fictional business, target audience, channel, and goal.
3. **Content calendar:** seven themes across one week.
4. **Two or three sample captions:** show variety, not just repeated templates.
5. **Visual direction:** explain what kind of approved image would support each post.
6. **Quality process:** fact checks, claim checks, image permissions, and client approval.
7. **Offer:** deliverables, one revision round, timeline, and contact method.

## How to turn a sample into a real case study

Only after a real client has agreed:
- Get permission to show the work and business name/logo.
- Describe exactly what you delivered.
- Include outcomes only if the client provided or approved verifiable data.
- Do not imply the content caused sales or growth unless that conclusion is supported.
