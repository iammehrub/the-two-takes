About Simple Digital Toolkit

Simple Digital Toolkit is a small independent learning project that publishes beginner-friendly tutorials about digital tools, creator workflows, and studying with limited budgets.

Our goal is to explain practical steps clearly, identify limitations, and favour free options when they are appropriate. We aim to update guides when tools change, but readers should verify current details in official documentation.

This site is not affiliated with YouTube, GitHub, or any other named service. We do not promise earnings, views, grades, or other outcomes.

Editorial approach

  • Prefer useful, original explanations over copied content.
  • Disclose limitations and potential costs.
  • Link to official documentation for changing technical details.
  • Correct mistakes when they are identified.
