# 7-Day Content Draft Pack — Sample Local Cafe

**Status:** FICTIONAL SAMPLE — NOT A REAL CLIENT RESULT  
**Location:** Dhaka, Bangladesh  
**Channel:** Facebook  
**Offer:** coffee, tea, and light snacks  
**Audience:** local residents and students

All items below are drafts for portfolio practice. Confirm menu, prices, opening hours, availability, and brand details with a real business before use.

## Day 1 — Meet the cafe
**Purpose:** Introduce the fictional business.

**Caption draft:**  
Meet Sample Local Cafe, a fictional neighborhood cafe concept in Dhaka. We imagine a welcoming place for coffee, tea, and light snacks. When you choose a spot to meet or take a break, what matters most to you: the menu, the atmosphere, or convenience? Share your preference. Message the business to confirm current details.

**Visual direction:** Use a real, owner-approved photo of the premises only after this becomes a real client project.

**Approval checklist:** Verify all facts; edit tone; confirm visual rights; approve before posting.

## Day 2 — Behind the scenes
**Purpose:** Suggest a genuine behind-the-scenes post without inventing a story.

**Caption draft:**  
A behind-the-scenes post works best when it shows something real. Sample Local Cafe could share an approved photo of drink preparation or the team getting ready, with a short explanation of what viewers are seeing. What would you like to see behind the counter? Contact the business for confirmed details.

**Visual direction:** Ask the owner for an approved original photo. Do not use a stock image as if it depicts the cafe.

## Day 3 — Offer spotlight
**Purpose:** Invite questions about the core offer.

**Caption draft:**  
Coffee, tea, and light snacks are the focus of this fictional cafe concept. If you are considering a cafe visit, what would you want to know before choosing? Ask about the current menu and details directly with the business.

**Visual direction:** Use an approved menu or product photo only after menu details are confirmed.

## Day 4 — Helpful checklist
**Purpose:** Provide useful decision-making guidance.

**Caption draft:**  
Planning to meet a friend at a cafe? A quick checklist can help: choose a convenient location, check the current menu, confirm opening hours, and agree on a time that works for everyone. Save this reminder for your next catch-up. Confirm current details directly with the cafe.

**Visual direction:** Simple checklist graphic with no unverified claims.

## Day 5 — Questions to ask
**Purpose:** Help customers confirm practical details.

**Caption draft:**  
Before heading to any cafe, it can help to confirm the current menu, opening hours, seating arrangements, and payment options. Which detail do you usually check first? Ask the business directly for accurate, up-to-date information.

**Visual direction:** Clear text-led graphic or approved cafe photo.

## Day 6 — Community question
**Purpose:** Encourage a low-pressure conversation.

**Caption draft:**  
Cafe question: when meeting friends, do you prefer a quiet catch-up, a quick tea break, or trying something new from the menu? Tell us what makes a cafe visit enjoyable for you. Check directly with the business for its current menu and details.

**Visual direction:** Use a simple question card or owner-approved photo.

## Day 7 — Weekly reminder
**Purpose:** Remind people to confirm details before visiting.

**Caption draft:**  
Planning a cafe visit this week? Make a plan, check the current menu and opening hours, and confirm any details you need before setting off. Sample Local Cafe is a fictional portfolio example; real business details must be verified with the owner before this caption is adapted.

**Visual direction:** Calendar-style graphic, clearly adapted for the real client's confirmed details.
