# Starter Offer: Weekly Social Content Draft Pack

## One-line offer

I help small businesses prepare a week's worth of social-media content drafts so the owner spends less time starting from a blank page.

## Pilot deliverables

- Seven caption drafts tailored to the business profile.
- A simple seven-day content calendar.
- Suggested visual direction for each post.
- A review checklist to catch unconfirmed prices, offers, dates, and claims.
- One revision round based on the client's feedback.

## Not included in the first pilot

- Automatic publishing or sending messages.
- Paid advertising or ad-spend management.
- Guaranteed reach, leads, sales, or follower growth.
- Fabricated reviews, testimonials, business facts, or performance claims.
- Use of copyrighted images or client data without appropriate permission.

## How the pilot works

1. Ask the client to complete `client-intake.md`.
2. Agree on deliverables, deadlines, revision limits, price, and payment terms in writing before starting.
3. Generate a draft pack from confirmed business information.
4. Review every draft internally for factual and brand risks.
5. Send the pack to the client for approval.
6. Make the agreed revisions and deliver the final files.
7. Ask for feedback and compare delivery time/quality with the client's previous process.

## Pricing

Quote only after understanding the workload and revision requirements. For the first pilot, keep the scope small and agree on a fixed fee before work begins. Do not imply that any sample is a real client result.

## Honest positioning

This is a content-preparation service supported by automation, not a promise of fully autonomous marketing. The client retains final approval over public posts and commercial claims.
