# Prospect Message Drafts — Review Before Sending

These are templates only. They are not sent automatically. Personalize each message using information you have permission to use, and do not mass-message people or claim you have audited a business when you have not.

## Short first message

Hi [Name/Business Team] — I’m testing a small service that prepares a week's worth of social-media caption drafts and a simple content calendar for [business type]. I made a sample format so you can see the deliverable before deciding anything. Would a short sample for [Business Name] be useful? No worries if it isn't a fit.

## Follow-up after they request a sample

Thanks — I’ll prepare a small draft using only the public business details you point me to. Please treat it as a draft, not a recommendation to publish; you can correct any details or ask me to remove anything. If the format is useful, we can discuss a small, clearly scoped pilot.

## After delivering the sample

Hi [Name] — the draft pack is ready: [link]. It includes [deliverables]. Please check facts, offers, visuals, and tone before using anything publicly. If you'd like to continue, I can outline the scope, timeline, revision limit, and fee before we begin.

## Before sending

- [ ] I have checked the recipient and business details.
- [ ] The message is relevant and not deceptive.
- [ ] I have not claimed a relationship, result, audit, or experience that I do not have.
- [ ] I have not attached private data or unlicensed assets.
- [ ] I have reviewed and explicitly approved this exact message.
