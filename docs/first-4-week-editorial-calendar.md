## Editorial rules

- Keep the title promise aligned with the actual lesson.
- The 4 Shorts must stand alone; do not post filler clips just to meet a quota.
- A Short may point to a related full episode, but it must still teach something on its own.
- Review the first 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days before deciding which topics to repeat.
- Swap future topics when fresh channel analytics show a clear, repeated winner or a repeated failure.
