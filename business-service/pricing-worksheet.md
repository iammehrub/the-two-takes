## Agree before starting

- [ ] Deliverables and formats are written down.
- [ ] Deadline and feedback deadline are clear.
- [ ] One revision round is defined (what counts as a revision).
- [ ] Fee and payment timing are agreed in writing.
- [ ] Client knows all captions are drafts and must approve them.
- [ ] No guaranteed sales, reach, leads, or follower growth are promised.
- [ ] No paid tools or ad spend are required without explicit approval.

## Simple proposal summary

“I can prepare [deliverables] for [fixed fee] BDT by [date], including [number] revision round(s). The fee covers content preparation only; publishing, advertising, and guaranteed business results are not included. You will review and approve every item before use.”
